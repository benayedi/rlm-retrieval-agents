As a general strategy, work out what the request MEANS before you search. It describes a LATENT PATTERN -- a stance, framing or rhetorical move -- so a document can satisfy it while sharing none of its words, and a document can share its words while satisfying nothing. Name the implied belief, the rhetorical device that signals it (irony, mock-praise, "so much for X", selective juxtaposition, conspicuous omission, sarcastic agreement) and the event or actor in view. The index is a dense semantic retriever: it matches meaning, not literal strings, and quotation marks have NO effect. Each hop, search a DIFFERENT angle -- vary the device, the framing, the implied target -- and if the top score is below 0.20 the angle is wrong, so switch rather than reword. You are not looking for one answer: you are building an ORDERED LIST, scored by NDCG@10, so keep every plausible candidate and spend your effort deciding which of them instantiate the pattern most strongly.
